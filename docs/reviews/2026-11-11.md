# Independent reviews — 2026-11-11

Three reviewers worked in parallel, each blind to the others: rules against the
design doc (R), bugs/netcode/security with a full secrecy audit (S), and
playability from real screenshots (P). Every finding below was reproduced with a
script or an exact code quote. Status is kept current as fixes land; each fix
comes with a test named in the Status column.

Severity: **H** breaks a core rule, crashes, leaks a secret or is exploitable;
**M** a rule implemented wrongly or a bug players will hit; **L** hardening/polish.

## Playability (P)

| # | Sev | Finding | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | H | Bless/Tut often hits the wrong cousin: the first cousin is locked from the start and a missed thin-limb raycast silently falls back to it (7 of 14 chest aims hit). | fixed: aim assist (closest body to the crosshair within 6 degrees or its width, line of sight, identifiable, sticky for 300 ms), no default lock (scroll cycles nobody, then each cousin), a ring under the target |
| P2 | H | Bots never stop sabotaging: 34–53 shoves, 7–16 plants and ~6 breaks per 3-minute visit; a human's item is stolen within 2 s of spawning. | fixed: each bot waits 20-30 s between its acts, nothing in a visit's opening, a human is not picked on again within 30 s (rarely in their first visit), at most 2 heirloom breaks a visit; ~3.9 incidents per bot per visit (was ~15) and 1.8 breaks (was 4+) — bots-pacing.test.ts |
