# Independent reviews — 2026-11-11

Three reviewers worked in parallel, each blind to the others: rules against the
design doc (R), bugs/netcode/security with a full secrecy audit (S), and
playability from real screenshots (P). Every finding below was reproduced with a
script or an exact code quote. Status is kept current as fixes land; each fix
comes with a test named in the Status column.

Severity: **H** breaks a core rule, crashes, leaks a secret or is exploitable;
**M** a rule implemented wrongly or a bug players will hit; **L** hardening/polish.

Creative ideas from the playability review (for the story-making work): "What Nana Saw" split replays at the reading; mishearing into plausible wrong phrases ("Love you, Nana" → "Shove you, Nana?") instead of symbol scrambling; Nana's diary read out by the lawyer next to the truth; corkboard with red string and a FRAMED! stamp at the reading; per-cousin "Nana thought / Actually" cards and a "Most wrongly blessed" award; Mittens as a witness who stares at the last saboteur; "Nana, look over there!" actually turning her head; a clock that chimes at false tattles.
